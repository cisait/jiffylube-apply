Privacy

What we collect

When you apply, we collect the store you pick, your name, email, phone number and your resume (a file or text you type in).

How we use it

We use it only to consider you for a job at our Jiffy Lube stores in North and South Carolina and to contact you about it.

Who sees it

Your application is emailed to the district manager who hires for the store you picked, and a record of it is kept in an internal hiring log. We don't sell it or share it with anyone outside our hiring process.

Questions or removal

To ask about your application or have it removed, email cisalubes@gmail.com.
